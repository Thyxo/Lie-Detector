How it works

One question. Zero guesswork.

“Do you have the magnet in your hand?” Whatever they answer, you already know the truth.

  1. 1

    Ask the question

    Hand out a magnetic coin. Behind their back they hide it in a hand – or not. Tap the screen to start.

  2. 2

    Feel the answer

    Move the phone over their hand. Only you feel it: 2 bumps = magnet, 1 bump = empty.

  3. 3

    Reveal: True

    Press volume up. The screen turns turquoise with a sound.

  4. 4

    …or reveal: False

    Press volume down. Red screen. Caught!
